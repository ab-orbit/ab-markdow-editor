# Welcome to Scriba

![An Egyptian scribe sets down ideas beside the Nile](assets/boas-vindas.jpg)

**A page to begin. Room to go further.**

A passing thought, tomorrow’s list, the outline of a project. Scriba brings words, formulas and drawings into Markdown notes you can edit, organize and take with you. Start on your device, even offline. No Scriba account is required.

## From the first line to the next idea

Create a note and write. **Saving is automatic**, on mobile and desktop. Edit the formatted text to change a word; open the Markdown source to work with its syntax. On mobile, the pencil switches between reading and editing.

Give your note a title. Try a task list. Add an image. Your first page already has somewhere to go.

## A small workshop of possibilities

- <i data-lucide="pencil"></i> **Write and format.** Headings, lists, tasks, quotes, links, tables and code blocks, with visual or Markdown editing.
- <i data-lucide="sigma"></i> **Give reasoning a shape.** Mathematical formulas and LaTeX, inline or displayed on their own.
- <i data-lucide="workflow"></i> **Connect ideas.** Mermaid and ZenUML diagrams to explore processes, relationships and architectures.
- <i data-lucide="pen-tool"></i> **Think through drawing.** Excalidraw inside notes and a Whiteboard workspace for editable boards.
- <i data-lucide="search"></i> **Find and organize.** Search your notes and pin the ones you want close at hand.
- <i data-lucide="list-tree"></i> **See the path.** Navigate headings in the outline and check statistics, including word count and estimated reading time, in the information panel.
- <i data-lucide="folder-open"></i> **Bring work in; take it along.** Import Markdown and ZIP packages. Export notes and images; drawings offer SVG, PNG or their available editable format.
- <i data-lucide="languages"></i> **Make yourself at home.** Portuguese, English, German and French, with light or dark themes. Choose your language in Settings.

## Open the atlas, change the map

The resource note **The Atlas of Possible Tomorrows** — file `The_Atlas_of_Possible_Tomorrows_Scriba_EN.md` — contains editable examples of text, mathematics, diagrams and drawings. Look for it among your pinned notes.

Choose an example, change a word or a connection, and watch what happens. This is an experimental notebook: scenarios and numbers are illustrative, and each renderer has its own capabilities.

## Your notebook, your destination

Notes begin in this app’s or browser’s private storage. In **Settings**, choose a folder where supported, or connect **GitHub** and select a repository. Its default notes folder is `scriba`; advanced options let you change it. Synchronization runs while Scriba is open and connected.

Offline changes stay on this device. Keep ZIP backups. Clearing browser data or uninstalling the app may remove private copies; files already exported or synchronized remain at their destination. **Files sent to a public repository become public.**
