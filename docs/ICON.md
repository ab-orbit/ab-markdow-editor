# Ícone Escriba

Arte original gerada com OpenAI ImageGen para esta aplicação: um escriba egípcio escrevendo em papiro, com azul profundo/lápis-lazúli e laranja inspirados no logo do SPHINX. Fonte: [scribe-source.png](../public/icons/scribe-source.png). A versão de 1024 px alimenta o `.icns` do Mac; favicon, Apple Touch Icon e ícones PWA são derivados da mesma arte. As variantes maskable recebem margem adicional.

Prompt utilizado:

> Create a production-quality square app icon, logo-brand use case, for a Markdown writing app inspired by SPHINX. A dignified ancient Egyptian human scribe in a clear side profile, short traditional wig, holding a reed pen and writing on a small papyrus scroll. Strong sculptural geometric silhouette, simplified vector-like shapes, sophisticated negative space, instantly legible at 32px. Deep midnight navy and lapis blue for the scribe (#102231 and #142b56), vivid warm orange (#ff7a18) for the reed pen and one small accent. Warm ivory papyrus (#f1eee5). Solid deep midnight navy square background, no rounded outer frame (OS will mask), no text, no letters, no watermark, no sparkles, no tiny hieroglyph detail. Center all meaningful shapes in the central 65 percent safe area, generous breathing room on all sides so the icon works in circular and squircle masks. Premium flat editorial emblem, crisp silhouettes, restrained subtle depth, balanced graphic composition, not a photorealistic portrait. The visual identity should feel related to an Egyptian sphinx logo with a navy face and orange feather, but clearly depict a human scribe with pen and papyrus, not a sphinx or pharaoh.

Para regenerar os tamanhos no macOS, execute `npm run icons`. O usuário final recebe os PNGs/ICO prontos e não precisa gerar imagens.
