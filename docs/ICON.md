# Ícone Escriba

Pictograma de um escriba egípcio sentado, escrevendo em papiro com cálamo laranja. Azul profundo/lápis-lazúli, laranja e marfim inspirados no SPHINX. A ilustração anterior tinha detalhes demais e perdia leitura nos ícones pequenos do Finder; esta versão prioriza silhueta clara e formas grandes. Arte gerada com OpenAI ImageGen; fonte em [scribe-source.png](../public/icons/scribe-source.png).

Prompt utilizado:

> Square app icon for a Markdown editor named Escriba. A very simple ancient Egyptian scribe pictogram: large ivory seated Egyptian writer in traditional Egyptian wig, with a bright orange writing reed and a rectangular ivory papyrus tablet. Flat bold geometric logo, like an Egyptian hieroglyph. Solid midnight navy background #102231, ivory #f1eee5 main silhouette, orange #ff7a18 writing reed, lapis blue #244b83 accent. Very high contrast. The seated writer silhouette occupies 85% of the square, minimal empty space. Maximum four large flat shapes; no fine details, no shading, no texture, no hair stripes, no text, no frame. Recognizable at 16 pixel favicon size. Prioritize the Egyptian profile silhouette and the large orange pen. Deliver only the icon, no mockup.

`npm run icons` deriva PNGs de 16 a 1024 px, variantes maskable, Apple Touch Icon e favicon. O instalador gera o `.icns` e o aplica ao aplicativo e ao alias nativo da Mesa.
