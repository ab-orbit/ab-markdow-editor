Scriba Privacy Policy

Updated on October 22, 2026. Scriba — Your ideas in Markdown, developed by Aeon Bridge Co.

Your notes

You can use the editor without creating an account. Notes, images, drawings, preferences and drafts stay on your device. On the web, they are stored in the browser’s private storage. In the iPhone and iPad app, they are stored in the app’s private storage. They are not automatically published.

Choosing a folder allows Scriba to read existing Markdown files and save notes and images in the scriba subfolder. The iOS app retains the folder authorization granted through Files. You can disconnect the folder in Settings. Exporting a file shares only the content you choose with the destination you select.

Optional GitHub connection

When you connect GitHub, Scriba’s synchronization service receives your account identifier and the authorization needed to access the permitted repositories. The session and credentials are encrypted on the server. In the iOS app, the opaque session identifier is stored in Keychain; OAuth credentials are not returned to the editor. If you choose to provide a personal access token, it is sent to the connection service and is not kept in web storage.

During synchronization, Markdown files and images pass through Scriba’s service to be read from or written to your GitHub repository. The service does not keep a document library on the server. GitHub retains files and commit history according to your permissions and the GitHub Privacy Statement. Files uploaded to public repositories are public.

Disconnecting GitHub removes the service session; it does not delete your repository. You can also revoke authorization in your GitHub account settings. Inactive sessions expire after seven days. Deleting a note on your device does not delete earlier GitHub commits.

Infrastructure

The website and synchronization service use HTTPS and Cloudflare. These services process technical connection data, such as IP address, request time and requested path, to deliver and protect the service. The web version may use technical metrics provided by Cloudflare. The native iOS package does not include an advertising, tracking or usage analytics SDK.

Scriba does not request contacts, location or microphone access, and does not read your photo library in the background. You choose images and files through system pickers; the camera is used only if you choose to take a photo to insert into a note. We do not sell your notes, use your content for advertising, or send drawings to Excalidraw or Mermaid servers for rendering.

Control and deletion

Export your notes and images before clearing website data or removing the app. These actions remove private copies from the device. Files saved to Files, GitHub or another destination remain there. Preferences, drawing drafts and private symbol libraries remain on the device until the app’s data is cleared.

Contact

For questions or requests about your data, contact contact@aeonbridge.com. Do not include tokens, private documents or sensitive data in a public request.
